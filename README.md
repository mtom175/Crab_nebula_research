The crab nebula is a pulsar wind nebula which means the winds of the central remnant drive the material and dust outwards, eventually dispersing the material over time. These nebulas display a range of high energy astrophysical properties, due to the charged particles and strong magnetic fields that are present.
I plan to use this repository to post any work I do surrounding the crab nebula, including any photos I take of the nebula. 
The nebula is visible during the winter period within the Northern hemisphere.
